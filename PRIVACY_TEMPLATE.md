# Privacy Policy — __APP__

_Last updated: __DATE___

**__APP__** is a bring‑your‑own‑key (BYOK) app. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own.

## What the app stores
All your data (entries, settings and your API key) is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What leaves your device
When you use an AI feature, the text (and photo, if you attached one) for that request is sent **directly from your phone to the AI provider you configured** (Anthropic, OpenAI, or any OpenAI‑compatible endpoint you entered), authenticated with **your** API key. Nothing is routed through us. Those providers process the request under their own privacy policies:
- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

The app never sends anything unless you trigger an AI action.

## Permissions
- **Internet** — required only to reach the AI provider you chose.
- **Camera / Photos** — optional; used only when you choose to attach a photo. Photos are downscaled on‑device and sent only as part of that AI request. They are not stored by the app.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
